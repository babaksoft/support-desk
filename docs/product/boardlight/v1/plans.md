# Plans and workspaces

Source ID: `BL-PLANS`

Version: `1`

## Product and subscription scope

Boardlight provides shared task boards inside workspaces. A person signs in with
one account and may belong to multiple workspaces. Each workspace has its own
subscription. Upgrading one workspace changes that workspace's plan; other
workspaces retain their existing plans.

The workspace switcher shows the current workspace name and ID. Its Settings page
shows the plan and membership. Check the workspace ID when two names are similar.

## Plan limits

| Plan | Boards per workspace | Members per workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 3 | 5 |
| Pro | 50 | 25 |

The owner counts as a member. Pending invitations reserve member slots. Once a
limit is reached, creating another board or inviting another member requires
freeing capacity or upgrading to a plan with sufficient capacity. Existing boards
remain readable and editable at the limit.

## Roles

Each workspace has one owner. The owner manages billing and invites or removes
members. Owners and members can create boards within plan limits and edit tasks.
Members who need an upgrade should contact their workspace owner.
